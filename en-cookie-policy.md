## 1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your browser when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to website owners.

## 4. How to Manage Cookies

### 4.1 Browser Settings

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings:

- **Chrome**: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
- **Firefox**: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
- **Safari**: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
- **Edge**: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies

### 4.2 Opt Out of Personalized Ads

- [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/) — Manage ad personalization preferences
- [Your Online Choices](https://www.youronlinechoices.com/) — Opt out for European users
- [Network Advertising Initiative](https://optout.networkadvertising.org/) — Opt out for US users

### 4.3 Opt Out of Google Analytics

Install the [Google Analytics opt-out plugin](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
